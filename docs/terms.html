The Application

The Application keeps a rolling buffer of recent audio on your device and lets you save, trim, transcribe and summarize recordings. Recording, on-device transcription and on-device summaries are free to use. The free version shows ads. Cloud transcription and other Cloud AI features are optional and use cloud credit, as described below. Older app versions may not offer every feature mentioned here.

You are responsible for using the Application lawfully, including getting any consent the law requires, and for how you store, use and share your recordings, transcripts and summaries. Do not use the Application to record people secretly where that is unlawful, or to harass, stalk or monitor anyone.

Accounts

You can use the Application without signing in. Signing in with Google is needed to sync settings and speaker profiles and to use cloud credit. Keep access to your Google account secure, because anyone who can use it can use your credit. You can delete your account at any time; see account deletion.

Cloud credit and payments

  • What credit is. Cloud credit pays for processing on our servers and our providers' services. The cost of each cloud job is shown in the Application before it starts. Local processing on your device does not use credit.
  • Starter credit. New users get a small amount of free starter credit, once per person. Deleting and re-creating an account does not give new starter credit.
  • Top-ups. You can buy credit in the Application. Prices, currencies and minimum amounts are shown before you pay. Payments are processed by Stripe, including Google Pay. Credit is added to your account when the payment is confirmed.
  • Auto Top-Up. Auto Top-Up is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, your saved card is charged the amount shown in Settings whenever your balance falls below the threshold shown there, at most five times in 24 hours. You can turn it off or remove your card in Settings at any time. A payment already submitted may still complete.
  • Failed jobs. If a cloud job fails, the credit it used is returned to your balance. Completed jobs are charged even if you are not happy with the result, because the processing has already been done.
  • Credit is not money. Credit cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred to another account or used outside the Application. Purchased credit does not expire while your account exists.
  • Ad-free benefits. Any ad-free time that comes with a top-up or from watching a rewarded ad is described in the Application before you choose it.
