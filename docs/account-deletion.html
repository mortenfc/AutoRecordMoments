Account deletion

These steps describe the current Auto Record Moments app. Older versions may use a different button label.

To permanently delete your Auto Record Moments account and the data synced with it, please follow these steps:

  1. Open the Auto Record Moments app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen (tap the gear icon in the top bar).
  3. If you are signed in, scroll to Account & privacy near the bottom of the screen.
  4. Tap the "Delete Account Permanently" button.
  5. Follow the on-screen confirmation prompts to complete the deletion.

Deleted: your sign-in account, synced settings, speaker names and voice profiles, cloud speaker samples, stored cloud transcripts and usage history, your credit balance, and any audio still waiting for cloud transcription.

Kept after deletion:

  • A one-way hash of your Google account identifier (or of your email address if the sign-in has no Google identifier) and how much free starter credit was left, so the starter credit cannot be claimed again by re-creating the account. It contains no audio or profile data.
  • Records of completed payments (Stripe payment ID, account ID, amounts and time) so each payment is credited only once, and any top-up that is still pending or awaiting manual support review. Stripe also keeps its own payment records.
  • A record of the deleted account ID and deletion-request time, so cleanup can finish and late payments or synchronization cannot recreate the account.

Recordings you saved on your phone are not affected; delete them from your chosen folder. See the Privacy Policy for details.

This action cannot be undone.

Request deletion without the app

You do not need to reinstall or open the app. Email mortenfjordchristensen@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request. Send the request from the email address you use to sign in and clearly ask to delete your Auto Record Moments account and associated cloud data. We may ask you to verify ownership before proceeding, and will confirm when deletion is complete. Never send your password, card details or recordings. The deleted and retained data are the same as listed above.
