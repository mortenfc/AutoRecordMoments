Make it work your way

These instructions describe the latest app. Available settings vary with your installed version, downloaded models and device.

Start with the basics

  1. Choose an audio preset and buffer duration in Settings, or start with Typical Use.
  2. Tap Start recording, allow microphone and notification access, and confirm the first-start guidance. The microphone starts only after confirmation.
  3. New audio replaces the oldest audio in the rolling buffer. Tap Save when something matters.
  4. Choose a folder when you first save. If you enable automatic saving, choose its folder before starting recording.

The buffer is temporary, not a permanent archive. Save what you want to keep. Automatic saving must be enabled separately.

Audio quality and buffer

  • Presets: Small Moments, Typical Use, Extended Buffer, and Music and detail provide starting points.
  • Buffer duration: choose hours, minutes and seconds to control how far back you can save. Longer buffers use more memory.
  • Sample rate and bit depth: balance audio detail with memory and file size. Settings shows resource and battery estimates, not guarantees.
  • Speech-only saves: optionally remove pauses, music and other non-speech and save detected speech at 16 kHz. This starts off for new settings and the Typical Use preset. Keep it off to preserve the original sound; other speech-focused presets may enable it.

Auto-save by speaker

Enroll a voice and enable speaker-triggered saving. Live voice matching happens on your phone.

  • Saved audio: keep full conversations with pauses and background sound, or save speech only.
  • Save when: wait for the selected speaker to go quiet, or wait for everyone to go quiet.
  • Quiet time: choose how long silence must last before saving. Shorter waits save sooner but may split conversations.
  • Join short conversations: set a minimum conversation duration and how long to wait for more speech. Set the joining duration to zero to turn joining off.
  • Recognition: adjust how long a person must speak and how many voice matches are required. More sensitive settings can also trigger on unwanted voices or TV.

Transcription

  • Only On-Device: process locally; download the required models first.
  • Fallback Cloud: try local processing first and ask before uploading if it needs help.
  • Always Cloud: upload when transcribing without asking again. Speaker scans may also upload audio.

Choose a recording language or automatic detection, speaker separation and its engine, and supported word-timing options. Choose the cloud model and optional word enhancement when using cloud processing. On supported devices, you can enable on-device transcription after saving.

Local processing does not spend cloud credits. Cloud transcription and optional enhancement use credits. Model availability and CPU, GPU or NPU acceleration depend on your phone.

Cloud credit and saved cards

Auto Top-Up is optional. When enabled, a manual top-up can save your card with Stripe for later charges at the threshold and amount shown in Settings. Review the card-saving notice before paying.

Turning Auto Top-Up off stops future automatic attempts but keeps the saved card. Use Remove saved card in Settings to request its removal and turn Auto Top-Up off. If removal is pending, use the retry action. A payment already submitted may still complete.

If a charge result is uncertain, the app waits for confirmation instead of starting another automatic charge. Credit can arrive after reconnection or backend reconciliation; do not assume a closed payment screen means payment failed.

Common questions

Why are notifications required?

Auto Record Moments requires microphone and notification access before recording can start. Its ongoing notification makes recording visible and provides pause and save controls, including from the lock screen. If notifications are denied, recording stays blocked: open Android Settings, choose Auto Record Moments, and allow notifications. This is an app requirement. Battery restrictions can still interrupt background recording; review the app’s recording info and your phone’s battery settings.

What happens when I clear the buffer?

After you confirm, unsaved audio in the rolling buffer is discarded. Files you already saved are unaffected. If recording is active, it continues collecting new audio.

Does deleting my account remove recordings from my phone?

No. Delete saved files from your chosen recording folder. Account deletion instructions explain which cloud data is removed and retained.

Why are features missing on my phone?

You may have an older app version or a device that does not support a particular model or accelerator. The website screenshot gallery shows the version 3.0.0 development build on a supported phone; older dated image files remain in the repository as historical assets.
